**Q1. (Multiple choice)** Two adjacent bases, CC, both change to TT in a melanoma. The best description is:

- A. Two independent SNVs
- B. An MNV, likely caused by UV light
- C. A 2 bp insertion
- D. A transversion caused by CpG deamination

**Q2. (Multiple choice)** Which of these is a **transition**?

- A. C>A
- B. G>T
- C. C>T
- D. A>T

**Q3. (Short answer)** A gene has 11 exons. Predict the consequence at the mRNA and protein level of (a) a nonsense variant in exon 3, and (b) a nonsense variant in exon 11. Name the mechanism behind the difference.

**Q4. (Multiple choice)** A 15 bp deletion inside a coding exon most likely causes:

- A. A frameshift
- B. An in-frame deletion of 5 amino acids
- C. A stop-loss
- D. No protein change, because the code is degenerate

**Q5. (Short answer)** TP53 is a tumour suppressor, yet most of its cancer mutations are missense rather than truncating. Explain why in 2–3 sentences.

**Q6. (Short answer)** A + strand gene has `AAAA` at c.100–c.103, and one A is deleted. Write the HGVS `c.` description, and state where a left-normalised VCF would place the deletion.

**Q7. (Multiple choice)** A balanced translocation between chr9 and chr22 is best supported in WGS data by:

- A. A step change in read depth across chr9
- B. Read pairs whose mates map to the other chromosome, plus split reads at the breakpoint, with no copy-number change
- C. Reduced B-allele frequency across chr22
- D. Many C>T substitutions near the breakpoint

**Q8. (Short answer)** Describe the two main ways a gene fusion can be oncogenic, with one example of each.

**Q9. (Multiple choice)** EML4::ALK in lung adenocarcinoma is most commonly formed by:

- A. A translocation between chr2 and chr5
- B. An inversion within chr2p
- C. Amplification of ALK
- D. A point mutation in the ALK kinase domain

**Q10. (Short answer)** Explain how variants at the MET exon 14 splice sites lead to an oncogenic MET protein, and why RNA sequencing is valuable for detecting this event.
