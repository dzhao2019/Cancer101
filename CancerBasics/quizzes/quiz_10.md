# Quiz 10 — The Transcriptome in Cancer

10 questions · ~20 minutes · closed book. Answer key: `answer_keys/answer_key_10.md`.

**Q1. (Multiple choice)** Which library preparation is usually preferred for degraded RNA from FFPE tissue?

- A. Poly-A selection
- B. rRNA depletion or exome-capture RNA-seq
- C. Single-cell RNA-seq
- D. Small-RNA sequencing

**Q2. (Short answer)** Why is TPM preferred over FPKM for comparing expression of a gene between samples?

**Q3. (Short answer)** WGS reports an SV with one breakpoint in intron 13 of EML4 and another in intron 19 of ALK. Name three things RNA-seq can tell you about this event that WGS cannot.

**Q4. (Multiple choice)** In a myeloma with t(11;14), RNA-seq most likely shows:

- A. An in-frame IGH::CCND1 chimeric protein
- B. Outlier high expression of intact CCND1, driven by IGH enhancers
- C. Loss of CCND1 expression
- D. MET exon 14 skipping

**Q5. (Short answer)** A TP53 nonsense mutation (not in the last exon) has DNA VAF 0.55 but RNA VAF 0.10. Give the most likely explanation. Why would a frameshift in the last exon of APC behave differently?

**Q6. (Multiple choice)** An A>G mismatch appears at VAF 0.35 in tumour RNA but is absent from tumour and normal DNA. The most likely cause is:

- A. A subclonal somatic mutation
- B. RNA editing by ADAR
- C. Germline contamination
- D. NMD

**Q7. (Short answer)** List three features that distinguish a likely real oncogenic fusion from a read-through or artefactual fusion call.

**Q8. (Multiple choice)** In a GBM sample, RNA-seq shows abundant junction reads joining EGFR exon 1 directly to exon 8. This indicates:

- A. EGFR amplification only
- B. EGFRvIII — an internal deletion of exons 2–7
- C. FGFR3::TACC3
- D. RNA degradation

**Q9. (Short answer)** A tumour suppressor shows no DNA mutation and no copy loss, but a germline-heterozygous SNP in its transcript is expressed from only one allele in tumour RNA (it is biallelic in normal-tissue RNA). What second-hit mechanism does this suggest, and why could WGS not find it?

**Q10. (Short answer)** Give three ways RNA QC failures or sample mix-ups could mislead a WGTS report, and a metric that would catch each.
