**Q1. (Multiple choice)** Spontaneous deamination of 5-methylcytosine most commonly produces:

- A. C>A at any context
- B. C>T at CpG dinucleotides
- C. T>A at TpA
- D. Single-base deletions in homopolymers

**Q2. (Multiple choice)** 8-oxoguanine is mutagenic because it:

- A. Blocks replication entirely
- B. Pairs with adenine, leading to G>T (C>A) substitutions
- C. Causes double-strand breaks
- D. Is removed by mismatch repair, creating deletions

**Q3. (Short answer)** Which repair pathway removes UV-induced pyrimidine dimers and tobacco adducts? Why do these mutations show transcriptional strand bias?

**Q4. (Multiple choice)** A tumour with loss of mismatch repair typically shows:

- A. Few mutations but many large deletions
- B. Many ±1 bp indels in microsatellites, plus elevated SNVs
- C. Ultramutation with almost no indels
- D. Only C>A substitutions

**Q5. (Short answer)** An MSI-high colorectal tumour has BRAF V600E, no MMR gene mutations, and very low MLH1 RNA expression. What is the most likely cause of the MMR loss, and what does it imply for the patient's family?

**Q6. (Multiple choice)** A microsatellite-stable endometrial tumour has an extremely high mutation burden dominated by C>A at TCT and C>T at TCG. The most likely cause is:

- A. Lynch syndrome
- B. Tobacco exposure
- C. A POLE exonuclease-domain hotspot mutation
- D. FFPE artifact

**Q7. (Short answer)** Explain in 3–4 sentences why PARP inhibitors kill BRCA-deficient tumour cells but largely spare normal cells.

**Q8. (Short answer)** A patient's BRCA2-mutant ovarian cancer stops responding to a PARP inhibitor. Biopsy sequencing shows a new deletion near the original frameshift. What has likely happened, and what would you check in the reads?

**Q9. (Multiple choice)** Mutect2's `orientation` filter (from `LearnReadOrientationModel`) is designed to catch:

- A. Reads aligning only to the reference + strand
- B. Damage to one strand of the original DNA fragment before amplification, which appears in only one read-pair orientation (F1R2 or F2R1)
- C. Variants in low-mappability regions
- D. Germline variants missed by the normal

**Q10. (Short answer)** An FFPE tumour shows thousands of low-VAF C>T calls. Give three lines of evidence you would use to decide whether they are biology or artifact, and one lab-side mitigation.
