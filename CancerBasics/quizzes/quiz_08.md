# Quiz 8 — Genomic Biomarkers and Mutational Signatures

**Q1. (Calculation)** A tumour–normal WGS has 18,000 PASS somatic SNVs and 2,000 PASS indels. The callable genome is 2,500 Mb. What is the genome-wide TMB? Name one reason this number may not be comparable to a panel TMB.

**Q2. (Multiple choice)** Why do MSI-high tumours respond particularly well to immune checkpoint inhibitors?

- A. MSI tumours overexpress PD-1
- B. Frameshifts in coding microsatellites create many novel, immunogenic peptides (neoantigens)
- C. MSI tumours have no HLA genes
- D. MSI tumours are always low purity

**Q3. (Short answer)** MMR IHC on a colorectal tumour shows loss of MLH1 and PMS2, with MSH2 and MSH6 retained. What two follow-up tests help decide between sporadic MSI and Lynch syndrome, and which results favour sporadic?

**Q4. (Multiple choice)** A tumour is ultramutated (> 100 mut/Mb), MSS, with few indels and dominant SBS10a/b. The most likely cause is:

- A. Tobacco exposure
- B. MLH1 promoter methylation
- C. A POLE exonuclease-domain mutation
- D. Homologous recombination deficiency

**Q5. (Short answer)** Name the three genomic scar components commonly summed into an HRD score, and explain why a tumour can be scar-positive but PARP-inhibitor-resistant.

**Q6. (Short answer)** How are the 96 SBS channels constructed? Why is a G>A mutation recorded as C>T?

**Q7. (Multiple choice)** You have one tumour sample and want to know how much APOBEC activity contributed. The appropriate approach is:

- A. De novo NMF extraction on that sample alone
- B. Fitting the sample's profile to known COSMIC signatures
- C. Counting only C>T mutations at CpG
- D. Measuring TMB

**Q8. (Matching)** Match each signature to its aetiology: (1) SBS4, (2) SBS7a, (3) SBS11, (4) SBS88, (5) ID6.
Aetiologies: (a) temozolomide, (b) colibactin-producing *E. coli*, (c) tobacco smoke, (d) HR deficiency (microhomology deletions), (e) UV light.

**Q9. (Short answer)** Describe the WGS appearance of chromothripsis, and how it differs from gradual chromosomal instability.

**Q10. (Multiple choice)** In a brain tumour, which arm-level pattern supports an IDH-mutant oligodendroglioma diagnosis?

- A. +7/−10
- B. 1p/19q codeletion
- C. 3p loss
- D. del(17p)
